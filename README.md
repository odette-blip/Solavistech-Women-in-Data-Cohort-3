# Solavistech-Women-in-Data-Cohort-3
Solavistech is a program that drills women who are interested in data and Artificial Intelligence. They also help women who wish or aim to change their career paths.
Being one of the women who are transitioning from a science field into this new Data path, i hope to have acquired by the end of this training the following;
The right mentorship on the specifics of my way forward. 
Gained basic and core knowledge on data learning, data analysis and artificial intelligence.
incorporated Proper disciplines and the technical/non-technical skills needed to forge ahead. 
Finally, i hope to connect with people and engage in activities that will provide me with the right opportunities throughout this learning phase.
